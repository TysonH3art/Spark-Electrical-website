About Sparky Electrical Services

Our History

Sparky Electrical Services was founded in 2015 in Johannesburg by a single qualified electrician working out of a small van. Through referrals and repeat customers, the business steadily grew into a trusted team of three qualified electricians, now serving homeowners and small businesses across the greater Johannesburg area. We specialise in residential and small commercial electrical installations, repairs, and Certificate of Compliance (COC) inspections.

Our Mission

To provide safe, reliable, and affordable electrical services to homes and small businesses in the greater Johannesburg area.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and recommended electrical service provider in the local community.
