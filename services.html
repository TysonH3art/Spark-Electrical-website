Our Services

Electrical Installations

From new plug points and lighting circuits to full house rewires, we handle installations for both new builds and renovations, all completed to SANS 10142 wiring standards.

Repairs and Fault Finding

Tripping breakers, faulty plug points, or flickering lights? We diagnose and fix electrical faults quickly and safely, with same-day call-outs available for urgent issues.

Certificate of Compliance (COC) Inspections

Selling your property or renewing insurance? We conduct full electrical compliance inspections and issue a valid Certificate of Compliance in line with South African regulations.
